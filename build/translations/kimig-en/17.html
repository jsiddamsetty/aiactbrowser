Authorities

(1) For the purposes of section 36(1), point (1), of the Act on Administrative Offences, the administrative authorities in the cases under section 15(1) and (2) and under Article 99(3) to (5) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 are the market surveillance authorities under section 2(1) to (8), the notifying authorities under section 3(1) and the first sentence of (2), and the bodies under section 3(4), first and second sentences, each for their own area of responsibility.

(2) No fines shall be imposed on authorities and other public bodies within the meaning of section 2(1) or (2) of the Federal Data Protection Act (Bundesdatenschutzgesetz) in the cases under section 15(1) and (2) and under Article 99(3) to (5) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.
