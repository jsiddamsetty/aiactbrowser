Provisions on fines relating to Regulation (EU) 2024/1689

(1) An administrative offence is committed by any person who, intentionally or negligently, infringes Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, in the version of 13 June 2024, by

1.

contrary to Article 21(1), failing to transmit information or documentation referred to therein, or not transmitting it correctly, completely or in good time,

2.

contrary to Article 21(2), failing to grant access,

3.

contrary to Article 27(1), failing to carry out a fundamental rights impact assessment referred to therein, or not carrying it out correctly, completely or in good time,

4.

contrary to the third sentence of Article 27(2), failing to update information, or not updating it correctly, completely or without delay, after having reached the view referred to therein,

5.

contrary to the first sentence of Article 27(3), failing to transmit a notification referred to therein, or not transmitting it correctly, completely or in good time,

6.

contrary to Article 45(1), points (a) to (c) or (d), failing to inform the notifying authority, at its request, or not informing it correctly or completely,

7.

contrary to Article 45(1), point (e), failing to inform the notifying authority, or not informing it correctly, completely or in good time,

8.

contrary to Article 45(2), failing to inform a body referred to therein, or not informing it correctly, completely or in good time, or

9.

contrary to Article 45(3), failing to transmit information referred to therein, or not transmitting it correctly, completely or in good time.

(2) An administrative offence is committed by any person who, as the deployer of a high-risk AI system under Article 6(2) in conjunction with points 1, 3, 4 or 5 of Annex III to Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, in the version of 13 June 2024, fails to ensure that an affected person is given an explanation referred to in Article 86(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, in the version of 13 June 2024.

(3) The administrative offence may be punished by a fine of up to fifty thousand euros.
