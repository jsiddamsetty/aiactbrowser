KI-MIG § 18

Retention obligations

KI-Marktüberwachungs- und Innovationsförderungs-Gesetz · Previous · Next

Where a provider within the meaning of Article 3, point (3), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 or an authorised representative established in Germany within the meaning of Article 3, point (5), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 ceases its business activity, the person responsible for the liquidation or dissolution of the legal person shall have the obligation under Article 18(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.
