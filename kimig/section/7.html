KI-MIG § 7

Notification in the event of non-conformity or risks of an AI system

KI-Marktüberwachungs- und Innovationsförderungs-Gesetz · Previous · Next

Official source ↗

(1) The competent market surveillance authority shall notify the European Commission and other Member States of the European Union, without delay through the Federal Institute for Occupational Safety and Health (BAuA), of any non-conformity of an AI system that is not confined to Germany, under Article 79(3), the second sentence of (5), and (7), and under the first and second sentences of Article 81(2) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.

(2) The competent market surveillance authority shall give notification, without delay through BAuA, of risks despite the conformity of an AI system under Article 82(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.

(3) Within the scope of its tasks, BAuA shall support the market surveillance of AI systems integrated into a product under one of the legal acts of the European Union referred to in Section A, points 4, 5, 7, 9 or 10, of Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 or under Regulation (EU) 2023/1230, in particular by providing evidence-based specialist knowledge for deployers, providers and standardisation bodies.
