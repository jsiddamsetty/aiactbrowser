Article 106

Amendment to Directive (EU) 2016/797

EU AI Act · Previous · Next

Official source ↗

In Article 5 of Directive (EU) 2016/797, the following paragraph is added:

‘12. When adopting delegated acts pursuant to paragraph 1 and implementing acts pursuant to paragraph 11 concerning Artificial Intelligence systems which are safety components within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council 7, the requirements set out in Chapter III, Section 2, of that Regulation shall be taken into account.
