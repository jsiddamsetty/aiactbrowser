Omnibus recital 4

EU AI Act · Previous · Next

Official source ↗

In order to enable AI innovation in the private and public sectors, it is important that the Commission and Member States’ competent authorities ensure that the supervision, enforcement and monitoring of sectoral and national laws do not create overlaps, inconsistent interpretations or divergent enforcement.
