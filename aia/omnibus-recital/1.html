Omnibus recital 1

EU AI Act · Previous · Next

Official source ↗

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council 5 lays down harmonised rules on artificial intelligence (AI) and aims to improve the functioning of the internal market, to promote the uptake of human-centric and trustworthy AI, while ensuring a high level of protection of health, safety and fundamental rights and supporting innovation. Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 entered into force on 1 August 2024. The entry into application of its provisions is staggered, with all rules entering into application by 2 August 2027.
