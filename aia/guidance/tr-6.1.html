Transparency § 6.1

Main components, concepts and related transparency obligation(s) for deep fakes under Article 50(4) AI Act

EU AI Act · Previous · Next

Official source ↗

(112)

Article 50(4), first subparagraph, AI Act applies if several conditions are fulfilled:

  1. The system must be an AI system;
  2. Used by deployers for professional purposes (see Sections 2.3. and 2.4.1.);
  3. To generate or manipulate image, audio or video content that constitutes a deep fake;
  4. The AI system must not fall under the exception for use authorised by law to detect, prevent, investigate or prosecute criminal offence.
