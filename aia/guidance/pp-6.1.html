Prohibitions § 6.1

Rationale and objectives

EU AI Act · Previous · Next

Official source ↗

(223)

The untargeted scraping of facial images from the internet and from CCTV footage seriously interferes with individuals’ rights to privacy and data protection and deny those individuals the right to remain anonymous. Recital 43 AI Act therefore justifies the prohibition established in Article 5(1)(e) AI Act based on the ‘feeling of mass surveillance’ and the risks of ‘gross violations of fundamental rights, including the right to privacy’.
