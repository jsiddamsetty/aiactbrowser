High-risk § 3.5

Access to and enjoyment of essential private services and essential public services and benefits

EU AI Act · Previous · Next
(279)

The access to and enjoyment of certain private and public services and benefits is an area in which the use of AI systems deserves special consideration . Such use may bring about benefits and improve efficiency and the quality of services. Such use may also seriously impact the access to and enjoyment of such essential services if the use of the AI system results in discriminatory or inaccurate outcomes or pose other risks to fundamental rights, as explained in Recital 58 AI Act.
