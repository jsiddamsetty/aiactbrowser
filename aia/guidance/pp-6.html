Prohibitions § 6

Article 5(1)(e) AI Act-untargeted scraping of facial images

EU AI Act · Previous · Next
(222)

Article 5(1)(e) AI Act prohibits the placing on the market, putting into service for this specific purpose, or the use of AI systems that create o r expand facial recognition databases through the untargeted scraping of facial images from the Internet or CCTV footage.
