High-risk § 2

Horizontal issues for Annex III use cases

EU AI Act · Previous · Next

This section provides clarifications on certain horizontal aspects regarding the classification of AI systems as high-risk under Article 6(2) AI Act, which are relevant for all areas and use cases listed in Annex III AI Act.
