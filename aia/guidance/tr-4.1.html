Transparency § 4.1

Main components and concepts of Article 50(2) AI Act

EU AI Act · Previous · Next

Official source ↗

(56)

Article 50(2) AI Act applies if several cumulative conditions are fulfilled:

  1. The system must qualify as an AI system;
  2. The AI system must be capable of generating or manipulating synthetic content;
  3. The content must be in one or more of the following modalities: audio, image, video or text;
  4. The AI system must not fall within any of the following exceptions: (1) the AI system performs an assistive function for standard editing ; (2) the AI system does not substantially alter the input data provided by the deployer or the semantics thereof ; or (3) the AI system is authorised by law to detect, prevent, investigate or prosecute criminal offences.
