High-risk § 3.8

Administration of justice and democratic processes

EU AI Act · Previous · Next

Official source ↗

(404)

Point 8 of Annex III AI Act identifies the administration of justice and democratic processes as one of the areas in which certain use cases of AI systems can pose significant risks to the health, safety and fundamental rights of natural persons . Recital 61 AI Act justifies the classification of such systems as high-risk due to their potentially significant impact on democracy, the rule of law and fundamental rights and freedoms, including the right to an effective remedy and to a fair trial.
